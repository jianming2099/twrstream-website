Last updated: September 16, 2026

This page explains, at a general level, how cookies and similar technologies may be used on the TWR STREAM website. The technologies actually used can change as website features are added or removed.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small data files that websites may store through a browser. Similar technologies can also be used to support functionality, remember settings or measure website activity.

2. Technologies used on this website

The website currently includes Google Analytics / Google tag code on certain pages to help understand website usage. Pages may also embed or load content from third-party services, such as hosted video or form-processing services. Those providers may process technical information or use their own storage technologies according to their policies and the way their services are configured.

3. Categories

Essential or functional technologies: technologies needed for website or form functionality may be used where applicable.

Analytics: analytics technologies may collect information about visits and interactions to help evaluate website performance.

Third-party media and services: embedded video, external forms or other third-party functionality may make requests to external providers when those features are used or loaded.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers provide controls to block or delete cookies and other site data. Restricting storage or scripts may affect some website functionality. Browser settings and third-party privacy controls are managed by their respective providers.

5. Personal information

For information about personal data submitted through inquiries, partnership forms and other website interactions, see the Privacy Policy.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website technologies, service providers or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the current published version.
