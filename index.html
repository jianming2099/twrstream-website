Who We Are

Built to move product.
Built to build brands.

TowerStream Interactive, LLC is a US-registered trade company operating three independent consumer brands across accessories, everyday essentials, and fashion. We work directly with manufacturers, which means faster lead times, flexible minimums, and no inflated costs from extra layers in the chain. Each brand is designed for the European and American market from the ground up: TETCHY for what you carry, LOOPBOSS for what you use every day, ShaiNewi for what you give and keep. Whether you're a wholesale buyer or a content creator, you're talking to the people who make the decisions.

Factory-Direct Supply
