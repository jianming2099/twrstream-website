How We Work

Cooperation models.

OEM

Original Equipment Manufacturing

We manufacture to your specification. You bring the design, we deliver production-ready product at scale. Ideal for brands that have already validated a product concept and want to bring it to market under their own label.

ODM

Original Design Manufacturing

We design and manufacture. You choose from our existing product portfolio or brief us on a category, and our team develops a ready-to-sell product to your market requirements. Faster to market, lower R&D cost.

Private Label

Your Brand, Our Product

Take our proven SKUs and sell them under your brand. We handle custom packaging, labeling, and compliance documentation. A low-risk way to extend a product range without building from scratch.
