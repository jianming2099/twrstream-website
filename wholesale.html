Terms at a Glance

Wholesale terms.

MOQ Minimum order quantities vary by product. Most SKUs start at 50 units. Volume pricing begins at 200 units. Contact us for exact figures per item.
Payment Terms 50% deposit on order confirmation, 50% prior to shipment. Wire transfer (TT), PayPal, and USD / EUR / GBP accepted. Net 30 available for established accounts.
Lead Time Standard stock: 25 to 35 days. Custom packaging or private label: 35 to 50 days. Rush orders subject to factory availability — ask at time of inquiry.
Shipping FOB Ningbo or Shanghai. Air freight available on request. We coordinate sea and air freight and can arrange door-to-door on select orders.
Sample Policy Samples available for all active products. Sample cost deducted from first bulk order. Lead time for samples: 5 to 10 business days.
