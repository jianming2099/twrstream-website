Brand Story

A bag with a
point of view.

"Not for everyone.
Probably for you."

TETCHY started from a straightforward frustration: most bags either look like they came off an assembly line, or cost more than rent. We believed a bag should say something — about how you move through the city, what you carry, what you stand for.

Every TETCHY piece draws from an Eastern design vocabulary — the negative space of mountain landscapes, the geometry found in centuries of textile tradition — translated into forms that belong in Brooklyn, Copenhagen, and everywhere between. The canvas is heavy. The thinking behind it is heavier.
