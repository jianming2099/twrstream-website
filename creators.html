Who We're Looking For

If you reach buyers,
we want to talk.

TikTok Creators

10K+ followers · US / EU audience

We work with TikTok Shop-eligible creators and organic content accounts. Strong engagement matters more than follower count. If your audience buys, we want to know about you.

Instagram Influencers

5K+ followers · lifestyle / fashion

Fashion, lifestyle, beauty, and home accounts with an engaged following in the US or Europe. We look at story views and link clicks, not just grid aesthetics. Niche audiences with real purchase intent are welcome.

YouTube Creators

2K+ subscribers · review / lifestyle

Product review, haul, and lifestyle channels that drive purchase decisions. We're interested in long-form product coverage and authentic storytelling. Subscriber count is less important than viewer intent.

What You Get

We keep it simple.

01

Free Products

We send you product to keep. No hidden cost, no obligation to post a script. You get the item, you create content on your terms. For first-time collabs, this is always how we start.

02

Commission

Earn a percentage of every sale you drive, tracked reliably through TikTok Shop affiliate links or dedicated discount codes. Payouts are prompt and transparent. The more you sell, the more you earn.

03

Long-term Partnership

For creators who perform well and fit the brand, we build something ongoing. That means repeat product access, priority on new launches, and potential paid arrangements as the relationship develops.
