What We Do

Manufacturing services for jewelry buyers.

One point of contact for development, production coordination, and supply — so your team can focus on design and sales.

OEM / ODM Manufacturing

Production developed from your tech packs, sketches, or reference samples. We coordinate manufacturing in China to your specifications, with pre-production samples for your approval before bulk runs.

Private-Label Production

Your brand on the product and the packaging. We coordinate private-label runs of existing or newly developed designs, with labeling and packaging options arranged per project.

Custom Jewelry Development

New designs developed together — from your concept or reference through sampling. Share drawings, photos, or physical references and we will coordinate development with manufacturers.

China Sourcing Coordination

Looking for the right factory? We identify and coordinate qualified jewelry manufacturers in China on your behalf, and manage communication, sampling, and production follow-up in English.

Wholesale Supply

Ready-made collections available for wholesale purchase. Browse our jewelry collection to evaluate quality firsthand, then order for resale.

Sampling & Quality Follow-Up

Pre-production samples for your review and approval as standard practice. Production progress and quality checkpoints are coordinated and reported per project.

FAQ

Common questions.

What do you need from me to start an RFQ?

Designs, reference images, or even a rough description — plus your target materials, estimated quantities, and destination country. We identify the missing details together during review.

Do you have minimum order quantities?

MOQs vary by product, material, and customization level, and are confirmed per project after reviewing your RFQ. Share your estimated quantities and we will quote accordingly.

Can I get samples before committing to production?

Yes — pre-production samples for your review and approval are standard practice before any bulk production commitment.

How do we communicate during the project?

In English, with one point of contact from RFQ to delivery — by email at jaymen@twrstream.com or WhatsApp.
