Our Story

Eastern craftsmanship meets contemporary expression.

ShaiNewi was built on a belief that the most meaningful pieces carry history. Each design draws from a tradition of East Asian craft — jade, silver, hand-finished detail — reinterpreted in a language that belongs to a woman who knows her own style. Not trend-driven. Not explained. Just worn.

01

Rooted in Craft

Every piece connects to a material tradition with real history. Jade, silver work, hand-finished detail — chosen because the craft earns its place, not because it photographs well.

02

Worn Daily

Made to be on you, not stored in a box. Designs that hold up to everyday life and look right in the morning, at the meeting, and on a Saturday night.

03

A Story Worth Telling

When someone asks where you got it, there is an answer. That is the whole point. Not just something you own — something you can speak to.

Material Integrity

Stone, silver, and seed — chosen for how they feel in the hand and hold up over years, not seasons.

Contemporary Sensibility

Eastern roots, current eye. The silhouettes are for today's woman — not a museum, not a gift shop.

Quiet Confidence

No logos. No explanation required. The piece speaks; you wear it with everything, and it holds its ground.
