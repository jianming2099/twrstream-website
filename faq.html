About TowerStream Company

TowerStream Interactive, LLC is a US-registered trade company that operates three independent consumer brands: TETCHY (canvas carry goods), LOOPBOSS (everyday home essentials), and ShaiNewi (jewelry and apparel). We design products for the European and American market and sell through platforms including TikTok Shop and Amazon, as well as direct wholesale channels.

We are incorporated in the United States. Our manufacturing relationships are based in Zhejiang Province, China. All trade documentation, invoicing, and communication is handled in English. We invoice in USD, EUR, and GBP.

TETCHY focuses on canvas totes, commuter bags, and carry accessories built for everyday use. The aesthetic is functional and design-forward.

LOOPBOSS covers everyday home and lifestyle essentials: practical goods that solve real problems without looking out of place in a well-designed space.

ShaiNewi is our jewelry and apparel brand, focused on refined personal pieces that are designed to be gifted or kept. It skews toward the European and North American fashion buyer.

Our products are available on TikTok Shop and Amazon. You can find links directly on each brand's page on this site. If you are a retailer or distributor looking to carry our products, see the wholesale section below.

Shipping & Logistics Delivery

We ship to the United States and across the European Union, as well as the United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. If your destination is not listed, contact us at jaymen@twrstream.com.

Lead times vary by product and order size. For in-stock inventory, orders typically ship within 5–10 business days. For made-to-order or custom quantities, production lead times range from 3 to 6 weeks.

Standard terms are Ex Works (EXW) or FOB, meaning customs clearance and import duties on the buyer's end are the buyer's responsibility. We provide all necessary documentation: commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin where required.

Yes. Tracking information is provided for all shipments once dispatched. For large freight shipments, we coordinate directly with the freight forwarder to keep you updated at each stage of transit.

Creator Collaboration Partnerships

We work with TikTok creators, Instagram accounts, and YouTube channels that reach buyers in the US and Europe. We consider all account sizes — micro-influencers with highly engaged niche audiences are often a better fit for our brands than large accounts with broad, less targeted followings.

We offer four formats: Seeding (free products, no posting obligation), Affiliate (tracked commission on every sale you drive), Paid (fixed-fee arrangements for proven creators), and Ambassador (ongoing partnership with a mix of product, commission, and paid arrangements).

No hard minimum. We evaluate engagement rate, content quality, and audience fit more than raw follower count. Apply through our Creators page and we will review your application on its merits.
